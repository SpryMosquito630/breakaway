# Breakaway

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Soccer players building an individual practice routine, using a phone beside a safe practice area. The player may have only a ball and a small space.

## Product Purpose

Turn a player's focus, experience, schedule, equipment, and space into a repeatable starter session. Let the player practice with a timer, adjust drills, and record their own practice and benchmark history.

## Positioning

A practical starting point for consistent, independent soccer practice. This is a local, account-free app, with illustrative training content rather than a coach-vetted program.

## Brand Personality

Direct, specific, encouraging. The user-selected Breakaway design system supplies the visual identity. Encouragement acknowledges actual practice without invented scores or claims about ability.

## Design Principles

The next useful action is easy to find. Setup constraints are respected by the plan. Practice stays under the player's control. Only elapsed practice becomes logged time. Personal results are distinguishable from guidance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable outdoor contrast, scalable text, controls at least 44 points (48 preferred), explicit labels and statuses, safe-area handling, and Reduce Motion support. No sound-only, color-only, or gesture-only essential interactions. The current build deliberately uses the reference's light theme.
