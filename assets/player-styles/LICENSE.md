# Player style photo attribution

These photographs illustrate optional playing-style inspirations. The subjects are not affiliated with or endorsing Breakaway. Names and style descriptions identify the inspirations; photos and questionnaire answers do not establish a user’s ability.

## Visible action photos

Every source was checked through exact Wikimedia Commons file-page `imageinfo` / `extmetadata`, then visually reviewed. Photos are bundled locally; there are no remote image dependencies or generated faces.

## Transformations and distribution

The nineteen action photos were cropped from genuine source photography, resized to 600×750 (0.80 aspect), and encoded as optimized progressive JPEG quality 84. No objects, faces, or background scenery were added or generated. The exact source crop fractions, downloaded dimensions, source dimensions, URLs, byte sizes, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. Centered cover rendering works at 273×341 and the narrower 224×299 viewport; the source framing preserves head and whole ball. Some secondary limbs or other players are cropped, and Neuer’s lower feet are outside his frame.

Preserve creator credit, source link, license link, and the cropping/resizing/compression notice in the app’s photo credits. CC BY-SA adaptations retain the exact same share-alike license stated per image; CC BY adaptations retain attribution. These image licenses do not relicense unrelated app source code. Copyright licenses do not establish a model release or player endorsement; source-page personality-rights notices remain applicable.

Balls are generally low in the frame. An opaque bottom fade can obscure them despite correct geometry, so final UI overlay verification remains necessary. The assets themselves include the visible balls; no dark fade is baked into the JPEGs.
