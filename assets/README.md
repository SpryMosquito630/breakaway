# Breakaway asset provenance

## Training editorial image

`training-editorial.webp` is an original AI-generated decorative image created November 4, 2026 with OpenAI image generation. It is not a third-party editorial photograph, a real player portrait, a coach-approved demonstration, or evidence of measured technique. The subject has no identifiable face, text or logos. No stock photographer attribution is claimed. Use is governed by the applicable OpenAI service terms; this is not a claim of a third-party stock license.

The original generation was 1086 × 1448 PNG. The app asset is a 900px-wide WebP, quality 82, approximately 148 KiB. It is cropped toward the ball and boots on Train and shown taller during onboarding. Original prompt: an adult player cropped waist-to-boots in charcoal kit, plain football, green grass, natural morning light, no brands or lettering.

## Drill training photographs

`drill-photos/` contains five licensed Pexels training-context photographs, mapped explicitly to 28 ground-work starter drills. The two verified demo posters take precedence in the interface for sole rolls and marker slalom. Context photographs are not exact-drill instruction frames or filmed demonstrations. Details offer an explicit Photo / Movement guide switch; only actual films receive video badges or appear under the Video demos filter. Exact authors, source/license links, crop dimensions, limits and hashes are recorded in `drill-photos/LICENSE.md` and `provenance.json`. Photo credit links are available in Instructions & notes.

Authored sample tracking data and notes must only be used with the matching source footage. No personal recording may inherit sample annotations.
