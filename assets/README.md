## Drill training photographs

`drill-photos/` contains 28 distinct genuine photographed references, with the two existing filmed posters completing real photographic coverage of all 30 built-in drills, including keepers. No built-in uses a generated or schematic thumbnail, and no photograph/alternate crop is shared across drills. A still illustrates a relevant setup/contact/handling phase rather than a complete drill demonstration. Reviewed local or online video examples receive video badges and appear under Video demos; an online badge does not imply a downloaded clip or an eligible embedded player. Optional movement guides remain authored instruction, never photo coverage. Exact authors, source/license links, crop/frame identities, quality limits and hashes are in `drill-photos/LICENSE.md`, `provenance.json` and `license-evidence.json`. CC attribution and changes are included in the Instructions & notes credit disclosure. Two wall preview frames are native 240 × 180; the L-turn macro is 440 × 330. Combined still-image payload is approximately 1.37 MiB. Thirteen drill references show documented professional/international action; specialized training references remain where they better illustrate the setup.
