## Gate dribble (`gates`)

Set four pairs of safe markers about a big step apart, with room between gates. Dribble through a gate, then change direction toward another. Use both feet and lift your eyes before choosing your next gate.

Capture: Find the next gate early.

## Push & follow (`push`)

Choose a clear spot a few steps away as your target. Gently push the ball toward it with the inside of your foot. Walk after the ball, stop it, and repeat with the other foot.

Capture: Pick a spot. Pass with purpose.

## Touch and freeze (`foundation-stop`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Make three inside touches between your feet, then stop the ball with a sole. Hold your balance briefly before repeating with the other stopping foot.

Capture: Three touches, one quiet stop.

## Shield and turn (`shield-turn`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Stand side-on to an imaginary defender with the ball beside your far foot. Roll it away with your sole and turn to follow without crossing your feet.

Capture: Keep your body between ball and space.

## Weaker-foot sole path (`weak-roll`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Use your weaker sole to roll the ball forward, sideways, then back. Keep your standing knee soft and reset before repeating the path.

Capture: Guide the ball with your sole.

## Pass and brake (`pass-brake`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Pass softly into a clear patch a couple of steps away. Walk after it and stop with the inside of the opposite foot. Alternate passing feet.

Capture: Weight the pass so you can catch it.

## Angled pass and collect (`pass-angle`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Set the ball out of your feet with one touch, then pass gently to a clear diagonal spot. Walk to collect it; alternate sides and keep the ball inside your area.

Capture: Set the angle before you pass.

## Pass through a gate (`gate-pass`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Place two flat markers a step apart with clear space beyond them. Pass gently through the gate from a few steps away; collect and alternate feet.

Capture: Aim through the middle.

## Carry and scan (`carry-scan`)

Check a clear, level area around you. Pick a clear lane and dribble slowly with the ball within one stride. Every three touches, lift your eyes to check the space, then stop and return.

Capture: Look up between touches.

## Keeper soft catch (`keeper-hold`)

Stand balanced on clear, level ground with room around you. Gently toss the ball from your hands only to face height. Catch it in front of your body with relaxed arms. Bring it toward your chest and reset. Keep both feet grounded; no jumps or dives.

Capture: Eyes on the ball. Soften the catch.

## Keeper roll distribution (`keeper-roll`)

Ask a partner to stand a few walking steps away in a clear lane. Lower the ball close to the ground and roll it gently toward their feet. Stay balanced. Your partner returns a gentle ground pass. Stop it with your foot, pick it up after it stops, then switch rolling hands.

Capture: Look at your receiver before rolling.
