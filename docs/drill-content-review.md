# Drill content review and filming queue

Status: not coach reviewed. Do not mark this complete without an identified qualified reviewer and dated written review.

For each drill and stage, a reviewer should check: safe setup/space/equipment, exact action and reset, appropriate repetition pacing, distinct progression/regression, weak-foot instructions, observable success criteria, and when to stop or rest. App-defined targets are pacing suggestions, not validated norms. Catalog changes should increase revision so prior Easy check-ins do not silently advance new content.

Filming priority:

1. Complete alternating sole-roll sequence (the current filmed example demonstrates one-foot contact).
2. Inside touches and touch-and-freeze foundations.
3. Receive-across and safe wall reception, filmed from an angle showing the receiving foot and open body.
4. Pull-back/inside/outside turns, then weaker-foot versions.
5. Passing-through-gate, slalom and partner receiving with space and setup visible.

Capture one full clean sequence for each actual stage, including setup, repetitions and reset. Provide normal speed plus slow replay where useful; avoid labeling a generic football clip as the drill. Use original adult demonstrations or footage with documented distribution rights and necessary participant permissions. Record author/source, date, rights and attribution alongside each asset. Review the final app crop and instructions against the clip before adding it to the catalog.

A human coach, filming and distribution permissions are external dependencies. No generated demonstration or automated review substitutes for them.
