# Training path content and limits

Paths are original, source-informed arrangements of the app's illustrative drills. They have undergone software/content consistency review, not review or approval by a qualified football coach, FIFA or a medical professional. They do not establish a player's ability, readiness to compete or fitness. Custom drills are user-authored and are clearly edited as such. No new filmed demonstrations are implied.

Primary references checked 2026-11-06:

- [FIFA — goalkeeping language](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/resources-tools/football-language/goalkeeping/index.php): contextual support for distribution with both feet and awareness of teammates.
- [FIFA — passing and receiving activation](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/talent-coach-programme/build-and-progress/passing-activation-playing-and-receiving-in-between-opponents.php): scanning before receiving and body position when receiving. The app uses original, simplified individual ball work rather than recreating the group exercise.
- [FIFA — fundamentals: learning to catch](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/environment/fifa-goalkeeper-training/goalkeeping-fundamentals/learning-to-catch.php): watching the ball and soft absorption on catches. The app deliberately restricts this to gentle standing self-tosses; it does not reproduce volleys, diving, jumps or shots.

The defender, midfielder and forward paths organize existing control, receiving, turning and passing tasks around those roles. They are ball-work pathways, not complete tactical curricula. The weaker-foot path uses slow controlled repetitions, not maximal effort.

Each path has three stages. Three completed sessions containing a completed path drill qualify for one stage; partial/skipped/zero-time results do not. Existing relevant practice is counted. Progression reflects logged practice, not evidence of technical mastery. The selected focus supplies up to two drills first; a selected path supplies supporting work. Equipment and space checks apply before every generated session. A manual plan remains authoritative. User-selected playlists are also validated at save, plan selection and practice start.

Daily check-ins only change today's gear/time. Low energy or a match day caps optional extra ball work at ten minutes; high energy adds no automatic volume. Match scheduling is a personal planning convenience, not a medical load recommendation. The seven-day review uses actual elapsed sessions, completed dates and optional self-reported effort. Two challenging reports suggest a shorter session for explicit user acceptance. No automatic makeup sessions or volume increases occur.

Limits: 40 custom drills, 20 playlists, eight drills per playlist, 100 favorite IDs; bounded text and revisioned custom-drill instructions. Active sessions freeze instruction snapshots. Deletion is blocked while a custom drill is referenced by an active session, saved plan or playlist. Existing history survives deletion. Optional malformed training fields are sanitized without invalidating legacy sessions/history.
