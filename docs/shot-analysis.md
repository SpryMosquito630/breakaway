# Shot analysis

The Expo preview Analyze tab is an account-free video review section. Laptop-backed analysis is excluded from release destinations. Players can record or select a video up to 30 seconds / 50 MB, replay at 0.5×, 1× or 2×, tap to seek, and step by 0.04 seconds. Playback pauses when leaving the tab, backgrounding the app, or opening the picker. Video duration comes from the loaded player; web and native picker metadata use different duration units.

## Default body tracking

Body tracking is available for every age range. The capture actions are explicitly labeled Record & analyze and Choose & analyze video, with local-processing and Google Gemini frame-sharing disclosure shown before selection. Newly selected or recorded clips start one analysis attempt automatically once playback metadata is ready. Body lines and labels start on. Failures and cancellation show an explicit retry beside the video rather than looping. No age or training profile is included in the clip request.

The current implementation sends the clip to the configured analysis service's MediaPipe pose endpoint. It does not send frames to OpenAI. The service deletes temporary files after processing. This development service currently runs on the developer's computer and is not production hosting; availability depends on that service and its network connection. Processing errors or cancellation leave the selected video available for local review. Cancelling stops the client request; an already running bounded server task may finish before cleanup.

A validated result contains genuine sampled 2D body landmarks. The overlay uses the video's current playback time, selects the nearest actual sample within at most 0.16 seconds, and omits low-confidence or out-of-frame points and any segment missing either endpoint. Video and skeleton share contain alignment; mismatched duration or orientation/aspect metadata rejects the result. There is no interpolated skeleton across missing intervals.

Body lines and movement labels can be toggled. Torso lean and Arm position identify visible body regions. Kicking knee and Support leg require an explicit kicking-foot choice and identify relative sides; they do not detect the contact phase, identify a backswing, or establish correct form. These landmarks are estimates, not technique ratings, injury assessment, speed measurement or skill scores.

## Local storage

Save replay explicitly copies the video to local replay storage. Pose results remain temporary component state and are not saved. Reopening a saved replay opens the video without stored body tracking or an automatic upload; its disclosed Analyze movement action can request fresh tracking. The existing limits of 10 clips / 200 MB, individual confirmed deletion, damaged-store recovery and Settings reset remain in force. There is no account requirement or cloud backup. Legacy tracked replays remain available as videos; the developer viewer distinguishes manual shot review from automatic ball tracking.

## Visible shot metrics

Technique and Shot speed appear side by side directly beneath the replay. Details and measurement setup open only when their metric is tapped; there is no new tab or separate results destination.

Technique is an explicitly subjective Gemini coaching estimate, not a validated technique test, OVR or body-tracking confidence. The isolated `/shot-review` endpoint samples at most 40 locally decoded/downscaled frames, identifies any supported shot style, and rates visible body control, striking action and follow-through against ordinal anchors. The server derives the /100 total. Missing phases, unknown style or low evidence confidence withhold the score rather than assigning zero. Every scored category cites an actual supplied-frame timestamp. The private paired-phone/account gate and existing AI quota apply before a provider request. When authorized, combined pose results avoid uploading the clip twice. If AI is unavailable or fails, ordinary body tracking remains available. No score is saved to Progress or advertised as measured improvement.

Shot speed is a manually calibrated estimated average: enter a measured straight-line ball-flight distance, then mark its endpoints using the actual paused player clock. The calculation is `3.6 × metres / seconds`. Blank/invalid distance, invalid or reversed marks and intervals shorter than the 0.20-second product guardrail withhold the value. This is not instantaneous/radar speed; curves and lobs may understate speed. Distance and marks reset with clip replacement and remain temporary. No additional upload is needed.
